7 Days Lemosho Route

Pricing

$1,995 USD

Per Person

Quick Facts

  • Duration: 7 Days
  • Difficulty: Moderate
  • Success Rate: 95%
  • Group Size: 2-8 persons

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1
Lemosho Gate - Mti Mkubwa Camp

Elevation: 2,100m to 2,650m | Distance: 7 km

Start your adventure from Lemosho Gate. Trek through the lush forest with diverse flora and fauna.

Day 2
Mti Mkubwa - Shira 1 Camp

Elevation: 2,650m to 3,600m | Distance: 10 km

Continue through the rainforest, emerging onto the Shira Plateau with stunning views.

Day 3
Shira 1 - Shira 2 Camp

Elevation: 3,600m to 3,850m | Distance: 5 km

Acclimatization day with short walk across the Shira Plateau. Prepare your body for higher altitudes.

Day 4
Shira 2 - Baranco Camp

Elevation: 3,850m to 3,950m | Distance: 8 km

Trek past Kibo Hut and cross the Baranco Valley. Excellent acclimatization route.

Day 5
Baranco Camp - Karanga Camp

Elevation: 3,950m to 4,050m | Distance: 5 km

Climb the Baranco Wall - the most challenging part. Rest at Karanga Camp.

Day 6
Karanga Camp - Barafu Camp

Elevation: 4,050m to 4,640m | Distance: 4 km

Final acclimatization day. Rest and prepare for the summit push.

Day 7
Summit Day & Descent

Elevation: 4,640m to 5,895m to 2,100m

Midnight summit push to Uhuru Peak. Celebrate your achievement and descend to Mweka Gate.
