Frequently Asked Questions

Which route is best for beginners?

The Marangu Route is often recommended for beginners due to its hut accommodations and established support. However, the 8-day Lemosho offers excellent acclimatization with lower success rate risk.

What is the best time to climb?

The best seasons are January-February and June-October. These periods offer stable weather and clearer skies. Avoid April-May (rainy season).

Do I need previous climbing experience?

No previous climbing experience is necessary. However, good fitness and acclimatization are crucial. We recommend regular cardio training before your trek.

What is altitude sickness and can I prevent it?

Altitude sickness occurs due to reduced oxygen at high elevations. Prevention includes proper acclimatization, staying hydrated, and listening to your guide's advice.

How much does a porter carry?

Each porter carries approximately 15-20 kg (33-44 lbs). We recommend bringing only essentials and letting porters handle your main luggage.

What should I pack?

Pack layers (weather changes dramatically), sturdy hiking boots, a good sleeping bag, water bottle, sun protection, and personal medications. We provide a detailed packing list.

What is included in the price?

Most packages include guides, porters, accommodation, meals on the mountain, park fees, and camping equipment. International flights and insurance are typically excluded.

Can I trek solo?

Solo trekking is not recommended due to safety concerns. All our treks are organized in groups. You'll meet fellow trekkers from around the world.
